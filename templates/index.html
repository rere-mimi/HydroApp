    DEM area of interest The orange polygon is the clip window. Upstream, downstream, and lateral distances set its edges about the pin. Adjust those values (and the centreline heading) until the rectangle covers the reach you want. LiDAR is not downloaded while you draw. When the polygon looks right, click Clip DEM to download each LINZ 1 m sheet the rectangle touches, then crop that window from those files. Each side can be 10–5,000 m. For 1,500 m × 1,000 m use 750 m up, 750 m down, and 500 m each side.

    Draw the orange polygon first. Distances are from the pin, 10–5,000 m each. The rectangle rotates with the centreline. LiDAR downloads only when you click Clip DEM.

    LiDAR window 500 m along × 500 m across.

    Flood hydrology Looks up the REC1 river reach nearest the pin on the NIWA Henderson & Collins flood statistics layer and shows the design flows. Matching 10, 50, 100 and 1,000-year values can be copied into the return periods below. There is no 25-year estimate on that layer.
    Return period (ARI) Annual Recurrence Interval. Select one or more design floods and enter the flow for each. Water level and velocity for every selected ARI overlay on the same profile, with the centreline slope shown on the long section.

    Select one or more intervals. Enter Q for each selected ARI. Profiles overlay on the same plot.

    Bed slope S Energy slope for Manning’s equation, as rise over run (m/m), not a percent. 0.002 is 1 in 500. Calculated uses the absolute slope of a straight line fitted to DEM elevations along the centreline. Specified replaces that value for the water level. The long-section line still shows the DEM fit.
    Elevation data

    New Zealand LiDAR 1 m DEM (LINZ layer 121859). HydroBridge downloads each Topo50 sheet the rectangle touches into outputs/linz-tiles, then crops the area of interest from those files. Coverage follows those LiDAR sheets, not a continuous DEM for all of New Zealand. Each screening run saves that 1 m crop in outputs/dems and reuses it next time.

    Clip DEM downloads the LINZ 1 m sheets that intersect the orange polygon, then crops the rectangle from those files. A site outside every indexed sheet, or on nodata inside a sheet, has no elevation. Nothing is fetched until you click Clip DEM or Run screening. The 1 m crop used for screening is saved under outputs/dems.
